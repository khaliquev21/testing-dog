## What the theme deliberately does not say

- No customer reviews, star ratings or review counts. The Reviews section tells shoppers that ratings appear once real reviews come in, and an app block can be added later.
- No performance results (for example "removes 95% of hair"). Third-party pages quote figures like this, but they are unverified marketing claims, so none are used.
- No shipping times, delivery promises, return windows, guarantees or warranties. The product page links to your Shopify policy pages instead.
- No tank capacity, battery life, materials, dimensions or other specifications.
- FAQ questions for "How does self-cleaning work", "How does the steam feature work" and "How do I clean the brush" are **hidden from shoppers** until you write an answer.

## Wording changes made to the approved copy

These keep the meaning and remove text that would be wrong on a live store:

- Bracketed notes such as `[Add your processing and delivery times]` are removed. Shipping, Returns and Support now link to your Shopify policy pages and contact page.
- Phrases that pointed at the supplier listing (for example "per the product listing", "as listed") were reworded to plain statements.
- The Hero, Product introduction and closing buttons show the live product price beside the label (for example "Shop the Brush | $29.99"). The price comes from the Shopify product. Turn it off per section if you prefer the original button.
- The step numerals "01 02 03" use the darker burnt orange `#C25B33`, already used for icons in the design, because `#E8754A` on ivory is only 2.8:1 contrast.
